Universal Converter

Convert a PDF to Word

Universal Converter rebuilds the text, headings, tables and pictures of a PDF as a Word file you can edit. Scanned PDFs are read with text recognition (OCR).

Download for Windows Free · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · Virus scan report
Mac (beta) Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later Android (beta) Android 10 or later

How to do it

  1. Open Universal Converter and choose Documents on the left.
  2. Drag your files or a whole folder into the window, or click Add files.
  3. Choose DOCX under Convert to.
  4. Click Convert. The new files are saved next to the originals, or in the folder you choose.

Why do it offline?

Universal Converter does the work on your own computer. Your files stay private, big files are no problem, and it works without an internet connection.

More things it can do

  • Compress a video without uploading it
  • Convert MKV, MOV, AVI and WMV to MP4
  • Save the sound of a video as MP3
  • Make a GIF from a video
  • Pull tables from a PDF into Excel
  • Combine photos into one PDF
  • Combine several files into one PDF
  • Copy the text from a picture (OCR)
  • Convert WEBP to JPG or PNG
  • Resize pictures or shrink them to a file size
