Universal Converter

Combine photos into one PDF

Useful for receipts, scanned pages or a photo album. Choose the page size and margins, and arrange the pictures with the arrows next to each one.

Download for Windows Free · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · Virus scan report
Mac (beta) Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later Android (beta) Android 10 or later

How to do it

  1. Open Universal Converter and choose Images on the left.
  2. Drag your files or a whole folder into the window, or click Add files.
  3. Choose PDF under Convert to, switch on "Combine into one PDF", and set the page size and margin.
  4. Click Convert. The new files are saved next to the originals, or in the folder you choose.

Why do it offline?

Universal Converter does the work on your own computer. Your files stay private, big files are no problem, and it works without an internet connection.

  • Convert WEBP to JPG or PNG
  • Resize pictures or shrink them to a file size
