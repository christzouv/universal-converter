Universal Converter

Compress a PDF to a size you choose

Add the PDF and type the largest size it may be. Universal Converter makes the pictures inside smaller, step by step, until the file fits, and keeps the best-looking version. Text and drawings stay sharp, and the original file is left as it was.

Download for Mac Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later
How to install and update
  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Universal Converter into Applications.
  2. Open the app. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't signed by Apple: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway and enter your password. You only do this once.

Updates: if you allow it when the app first opens, it checks for new versions each time it starts, verifies their digital signature and installs them when you choose.

Download for Android Android 10 or later
How to install and update
  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  3. Tap Install.

Updates: the app checks about once a day and tells you when a new version is ready. Download it the same way and install it over the old one; your settings are kept.

How to do it

  1. Open Universal Converter and choose Documents & PDF on the left.
  2. Drag your files or a whole folder into the window, or click Add files.
  3. Choose Compress PDF under Convert to, then enter the target size in KB or MB.
  4. Click Convert. The new files are saved next to the originals, or in the folder you choose.

Why do it offline?

Universal Converter does the work on your own device. Your files stay private, big files are no problem, and it works without an internet connection.
