Universal Converter

Convert MKV, MOV, AVI and WMV to MP4

MP4 plays on phones, TVs, browsers and editing programs. Universal Converter opens MKV, MOV, AVI, WMV, FLV, MPG, TS, WebM and more, and turns them into MP4, keeping the audio and subtitle tracks you choose.

Download for Windows Free · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit
Mac (beta) Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later Android (beta) Android 10 or later

How to do it

  1. Open Universal Converter and choose Video on the left.
  2. Drag your files or a whole folder into the window, or click Add files.
  3. Choose MP4 under Convert to. Leave the rest as it is, or pick a preset.
  4. Click Convert. The new files are saved next to the originals, or in the folder you choose.

Why do it offline?

Universal Converter does the work on your own computer. Your files stay private, big files are no problem, and it works without an internet connection.
